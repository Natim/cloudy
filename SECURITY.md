# Security

Please report security vulnerabilities in Cloudy or in this source tree privately.

Email [contact@cloudy.fr](mailto:contact@cloudy.fr). Do not open a public issue, pull request, or discussion for a suspected vulnerability, and do not disclose it publicly until we have had a chance to respond.

Include enough detail to reproduce the problem: affected URL or component, steps, and impact. A proof of concept is welcome. Please avoid exploits that destroy data or interrupt the service at [cloudy.fr](https://www.cloudy.fr/).

We will acknowledge the report and work on a fix. We do not run a paid bug-bounty program.
